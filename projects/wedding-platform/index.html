PROJECT CASE STUDY

Wedding experiences.

Interactive invitations, RSVP, guest details, and a dynamic SaaS solution shaped by real wedding-planning experience.

Interactive invitationsRSVPGuest managementSaaS in progress

Where it started

I have developed multiple custom interactive wedding invitations, RSVP experiences, guest-detail flows, and management tools. Each project began with the same goal: make a wedding experience feel personal while making the planning work easier to manage.

Why I am building the SaaS

Now that I am preparing for my own wedding, I am working on a dynamic SaaS solution based on our personal experience as a couple and the problems we are facing while planning. The product is being shaped around real needs rather than assumptions.

What it is exploring

  • Personalized interactive wedding invitations.
  • RSVP collection and guest-detail management.
  • Clearer coordination of events, guests, and important planning information.
  • A reusable product that can support the features couples actually need for their wedding.

Current status

This is an evolving product initiative. The next phase is turning proven invitation and RSVP experiences into a coherent SaaS platform that can grow with a couple from first announcement to final celebration.
