PROJECT CASE STUDY

Rashaqa AI.

An AI-assisted body-composition coach that turns scan data into clearer progress insights, training direction, and meal guidance.

Angular 21FirebaseGeminiVercel

The story

What if AI could help build an application and power the intelligence behind it? That is what I wanted to explore with Rashaqa AI, a personal project built through AI-assisted development.

It started with a simple problem: body-composition scans provide useful data — body fat, muscle mass, visceral fat, BMR, waist measurements, and more — but having the numbers does not necessarily mean understanding what changed, whether progress is happening, or what to focus on next.

I wanted to understand body progress beyond the number on the scale.

Two ways I used AI

01

AI as a development partner

AI supported interface design, implementation, feature development, and iteration while I shaped the product direction and validated the result.

02

AI as part of the product

Gemini turns body-composition data into clearer trends, progress summaries, and practical guidance for training and meals.

The product

Rashaqa is designed to help a user recognize whether they are losing fat, gaining muscle, maintaining progress, or moving in the wrong direction — then decide what to focus on next.

  • Body-composition tracking for key metrics over time.
  • Progress dashboards with comparison insights and custom SVG charts.
  • AI-generated summaries that translate changes into understandable language.
  • Workout guidance and meal-plan guidance based on scan trends and preferences.
  • A responsive, mobile-friendly experience with authentication, profile controls, themes, and account management.

Technical foundation

The app uses Angular standalone components and lazy-loaded routes, Reactive Forms, Firebase Authentication, Firestore persistence, RxJS, custom SVG charts, and a Vercel-ready SPA deployment. User data is scoped under each authenticated user's Firestore path.

Current status

Rashaqa AI is the first MVP. It is a personal project for experimentation and personal use, still being refined — especially around the reliability and usefulness of AI-generated insights.

Why it matters

AI is not only a feature in Rashaqa. It is also part of the process used to explore, build, and iterate the application.

Important note

Rashaqa is an experimental MVP. AI output may be inaccurate, is not medical advice, and should not be used as long-term data storage during testing.

Production consideration

The Gemini key is currently client-side for MVP development. A production release should move AI requests behind a server-side endpoint and add stronger validation and testing.
