The shift from automation to intelligent automation

AI is not just about doing things faster. It is about designing systems that understand and guide.

A few days after completing the One Million Prompters initiative, I realized something important: I was not just automating anymore; I was starting to think differently about the nature of automation.

This demo is a simple example. I previously built a system that was already fully automated, connected to my device, fetching transactions in real time, and structuring them into Google Sheets.

After the training, the shift became clear. It is no longer just about collecting or organizing data; it is about making that data think. By layering a custom-prompted AI assistant on top, the system does not just store information — it analyzes it, highlights patterns, and suggests actionable insights.

That is the difference between automation and intelligent automation.

Next step

The current project demonstrates the automated workflow. The next iteration is to add an AI layer that can interpret the structured data and provide useful, explainable recommendations.

All data shown in this demo is simulated for illustrative purposes.
